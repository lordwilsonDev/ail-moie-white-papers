# Launch Checklist

Phased rollout sequence for turning the blueprint into an operating
business. Each phase should be functioning (delivering, invoiced, and
referenceable) before moving to the next.

**Delivery constraint:** no live calls (no Zoom, no phone) in the sales or
delivery loop through Phase 1. Everything runs on async intake → done
delivery. See Phase 0 for the service selection and intake mechanics that
make this possible.

## Phase 0 — Zero-Meeting Async Services (start here)
Services selected specifically because the entire job can be scoped from a
form and delivered as a file, with no discovery call needed — the input is
a document/data/brief, not a conversation about the client's phone system,
call scripts, or live integrations.

Launch order:
1. OCR Data Extraction
2. AI Data Entry
3. Invoice Processing
4. Receipt Processing
5. Document Digitization
6. Spreadsheet Cleanup
7. PDF Summarization
8. Form Automation
9. Content Generation (blog posts, product descriptions, website copy)
10. File Organization / Digital Filing Systems

Intake workflow (no calls, either direction):
- [ ] Build one intake form per service (what to upload, format, turnaround,
      price) — a form, not a booked call
- [ ] Set up a shared-folder or upload link for file drop-off and delivery
- [ ] Revisions handled by email or a short async screen-recording (e.g.
      Loom), never a live call
- [ ] Publish fixed prices per service so no sales call is needed to quote

Explicitly deferred to a later phase (these need live/structured discovery
before they can be delivered well): AI Phone Receptionist, AI Voice
Assistant, AI Appointment Scheduling, AI Live Chat Assistant, Workflow
Automation, CRM Automation. Revisit these once a written (non-call) intake
questionnaire exists that can replace a discovery call.

## Phase 1 — Entry Services (low complexity, fast trust-building)
**Chosen launch three:** Invoice Processing, AI Data Entry, Spreadsheet
Cleanup. Picked over the other seven Phase 0 services because they are
recurring by nature (fresh batch every month, not a one-off), hard to
DIY (unlike PDF Summarization / Content Generation, which a client could
just paste into ChatGPT themselves), and bundle cleanly into a single
"bookkeeping cleanup" pitch to accountants/bookkeepers as a referral
channel.
- [ ] Define fixed-price packages for each (scope, turnaround, price)
- [ ] Stand up the 3 matching Google Forms (`intake-forms/generate_google_forms.gs`
      already covers all three)
- [ ] Land first 3-5 paying clients, sourced and closed without a call —
      target channels: local bookkeepers/accountants (referral), r/smallbusiness,
      local Facebook business groups, fixed-price Upwork/Fiverr gigs
- [ ] Collect at least one case study / testimonial

## Phase 2 — Automation & Virtual Employees (higher value, recurring-ready)
- [ ] Expand into Business Automation (Workflow Automation, CRM Automation)
- [ ] Launch one AI Virtual Employee Service (e.g. AI Phone Receptionist or
      AI Live Chat Assistant)
- [ ] Convert at least one Phase 1 client into a Phase 2 engagement

## Phase 3 — Recurring Revenue
- [ ] Package delivered automations as Subscription Services (hosting,
      monitoring, maintenance, updates, support)
- [ ] Move existing one-time-project clients onto a maintenance/support
      subscription where applicable
- [ ] Track MRR (monthly recurring revenue) separately from project revenue

## Phase 4 — Vertical & Consulting Expansion
- [ ] Package 1-2 Industry-Specific bundles for the verticals already
      served (e.g. Real Estate Assistant, Accounting Assistant)
- [ ] Offer AI Consulting (Readiness Assessments, Strategy Consulting) as a
      standalone entry point for larger prospects
- [ ] Use Business Intelligence services (dashboards, reporting, KPI
      tracking) to demonstrate ongoing value to subscription clients

## Phase 5 — Future Expansion (only after Phases 1-4 are stable)
- [ ] Evaluate demand for Multi-Agent Business Automation and specialized
      AI role replacements (Executive Assistant, Compliance, HR, Finance,
      Operations)
- [ ] Pilot with an existing, high-trust client before general release
