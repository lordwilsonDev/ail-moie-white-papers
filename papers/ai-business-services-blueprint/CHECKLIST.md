# Launch Checklist

Phased rollout sequence for turning the blueprint into an operating
business. Each phase should be functioning (delivering, invoiced, and
referenceable) before moving to the next.

## Phase 1 — Entry Services (low complexity, fast trust-building)
- [ ] Pick 2-3 services from Document & PDF Services or Administrative
      Services as the initial offer (e.g. OCR Data Extraction, AI Data
      Entry, Invoice Processing)
- [ ] Define fixed-price packages for each (scope, turnaround, price)
- [ ] Land first 3-5 paying clients
- [ ] Collect at least one case study / testimonial

## Phase 2 — Automation & Virtual Employees (higher value, recurring-ready)
- [ ] Expand into Business Automation (Workflow Automation, CRM Automation)
- [ ] Launch one AI Virtual Employee Service (e.g. AI Phone Receptionist or
      AI Live Chat Assistant)
- [ ] Convert at least one Phase 1 client into a Phase 2 engagement

## Phase 3 — Recurring Revenue
- [ ] Package delivered automations as Subscription Services (hosting,
      monitoring, maintenance, updates, support)
- [ ] Move existing one-time-project clients onto a maintenance/support
      subscription where applicable
- [ ] Track MRR (monthly recurring revenue) separately from project revenue

## Phase 4 — Vertical & Consulting Expansion
- [ ] Package 1-2 Industry-Specific bundles for the verticals already
      served (e.g. Real Estate Assistant, Accounting Assistant)
- [ ] Offer AI Consulting (Readiness Assessments, Strategy Consulting) as a
      standalone entry point for larger prospects
- [ ] Use Business Intelligence services (dashboards, reporting, KPI
      tracking) to demonstrate ongoing value to subscription clients

## Phase 5 — Future Expansion (only after Phases 1-4 are stable)
- [ ] Evaluate demand for Multi-Agent Business Automation and specialized
      AI role replacements (Executive Assistant, Compliance, HR, Finance,
      Operations)
- [ ] Pilot with an existing, high-trust client before general release
