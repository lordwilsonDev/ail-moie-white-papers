# Rationale

## Why categorize this way
The eleven categories in `BLUEPRINT.md` are ordered by increasing
implementation complexity and client trust required, not alphabetically:

1. **Virtual Employee Services** and **Document/PDF Services** lead because
   they map to concrete, easily-scoped pain points (missed calls, backlog of
   paperwork) that are cheap to demo and quick to deliver value on.
2. **Administrative Services** and **Business Automation** follow — once a
   client trusts the vendor with communications or documents, workflow and
   CRM automation is a natural upsell.
3. **Sales & Marketing**, **Customer Experience**, and **Business
   Intelligence** require more context about the client's operations and
   data, so they come after an initial engagement has been delivered.
4. **Industry-Specific AI Solutions** exists because most of the earlier
   categories are horizontal (apply to any business); vertical packaging
   (e.g. "Law Office Assistant" bundling PDF, document, and scheduling
   services) is a repeatable way to price and market to a specific trade.
5. **AI Consulting** sits deliberately near the end: consulting-only
   engagements (readiness assessments, strategy) are higher-trust asks and
   convert better once the business has delivery credibility from the
   categories above.
6. **Subscription Services** is the recurring-revenue layer that every
   other category feeds into — nearly any one-time project in categories
   1-9 can be converted into a hosted/monitored/maintained subscription.
7. **Future Expansion** is intentionally separated from the core catalog.
   These are higher-risk, higher-complexity offerings (multi-agent systems,
   AI roles replacing entire job functions) that should not be sold until
   the business has proven delivery on the simpler categories.

## Why two revenue tracks
A pure one-time-project business has to keep re-selling — it never
compounds. A pure subscription business struggles to acquire clients who
don't yet trust recurring AI spend. Pairing them lets a project (e.g. "AI
setup and implementation") serve as the sales motion for a subscription
(e.g. "AI Automation Maintenance"), so each one-time engagement is
evaluated for whether it seeds a recurring line item.

## Why start small
Data entry, PDF processing, and other low-complexity services in categories
2-3 are the intended entry point, not the target end-state. They are
low-risk for both the business (fast to deliver, low chance of failure) and
the client (small dollar commitment), and they generate the case studies
and referrals needed to sell the higher-value automation and virtual
employee services in categories 1 and 4.
