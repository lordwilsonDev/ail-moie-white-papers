## Business Model

### One-Time Projects
- AI setup and implementation
- Workflow design
- Document migration
- Staff training

### Monthly Recurring Revenue
- Hosting
- Monitoring
- Maintenance
- AI improvements
- Support
- Updates

### Growth Path
This catalog is structured to let a business start with small, low-risk
jobs — data entry, PDF processing, document digitization — and use those
engagements to build trust and case studies. From there, the business
expands into higher-value workflow automation and AI virtual employee
services (categories 1, 4, 8), which convert naturally into monthly
recurring revenue (category 10): hosting, monitoring, maintenance, AI
improvements, support, and updates. Category 11 (Future Expansion) marks
the next tier once the core catalog is delivering reliably — multi-agent
automation and AI roles that replace or augment entire job functions
(executive assistant, compliance, HR, finance, operations).
