# Intake Forms — Future Expansion

Common fields (business name, contact name/email/phone) apply to every form
below; not repeated per service. Per `CHECKLIST.md` Phase 5, these should
only be piloted with an existing, high-trust client — the form still needs
to stand alone with no call, but expect more back-and-forth by email before
scope is final.

## 1. Multi-Agent Business Automation
- Processes involved (write-in list)
- Systems to connect (write-in list)
- Goals for the automation
- Pilot scope (what's in / out for a first version)
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: pilot automation + evaluation summary

## 2. AI Research Assistant
- Research topics (write-in list)

- Output format: report / brief / ongoing digest
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: research output
