# Intake Forms — Subscription Services

Common fields (business name, contact name/email/phone) apply to every form
below; not repeated per service. These are onboarding forms for recurring
engagements — typically filed after a one-time project from another
category has already been delivered, converting it into a monthly plan.

## 1. AI Receptionist as a Service
- Reference: which delivered AI Phone Receptionist / AI Call Answering setup this covers
- Coverage hours needed
- Estimated monthly call volume
- SLA expectations (response time, escalation)
- Turnaround / Budget range (monthly)
- Delivery: signed subscription + monitoring dashboard access
