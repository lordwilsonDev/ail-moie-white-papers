# Intake Forms — Industry-Specific AI Solutions

Common fields (business name, contact name/email/phone) apply to every form
below; not repeated per service. Each of these typically bundles several
services from other categories (e.g. document processing + scheduling) —
the form below scopes the bundle; link out to the specific service form
(other files in this folder) for details on an individual component.

- Case management system used

- Confidentiality / privilege handling requirements
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: configured assistant + document workflow summary
