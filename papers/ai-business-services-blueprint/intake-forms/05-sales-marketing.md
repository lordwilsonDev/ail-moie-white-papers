# Intake Forms — Sales & Marketing

Common fields (business name, contact name/email/phone) apply to every form
below; not repeated per service.

## 1. AI Sales Assistant
- **Uploads:** product/service info
- Sales process steps (write-in)
- CRM used
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: configured assistant + sample interaction

## 2. Lead Capture
- Website/landing page URL
- Form fields needed (write-in list)
- Destination for captured leads: CRM / email / spreadsheet
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: installed capture form + test submission

## 3. Lead Qualification
- Lead source(s)
- Qualifying criteria (write-in, or **Uploads:** existing script)
- Scoring method (write-in)
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: qualification flow + sample scored lead

## 4. Customer Follow-Up
- Follow-up cadence desired (write-in)
- Channel: email / SMS / both
- Tone / brand voice (write-in, or **Uploads:** brand guide)
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: follow-up sequence + sample messages

## 5. Proposal Writing
- **Uploads:** past proposals or brand info, if available
- Project details for the proposal (free text)
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: written proposal document

## 6. Email Marketing
- Email platform used
- List segment to target
- Campaign goal
- **Uploads:** brand voice guide / assets
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: campaign copy + platform-ready file

## 7. Social Media Automation
- Platforms used (check all)
- Posting frequency desired
- Content pillars / topics (write-in list)
- **Uploads:** brand assets
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: content calendar + scheduled/queued posts

## 8. Content Generation
- Content type (write-in: article, guide, script, etc.)
- Topic list
- Word count / length
- **Uploads:** brand voice guide
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: finished content files

## 9. Blog Writing
- Topic / target keywords
- Word count
- SEO requirements (write-in, or "standard on-page SEO")
- **Uploads:** brand voice guide
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: publish-ready blog post(s)

## 10. Product Descriptions
- **Uploads:** product list / spec sheet
- Tone
- Length per description
- Platform: Shopify / Amazon / other
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: finished descriptions, platform-formatted

## 11. Website Copywriting
- Site pages needed (write-in list)
- **Uploads:** brand voice guide, existing copy for reference
- Turnaround / Budget range
- Delivery: finished page copy
