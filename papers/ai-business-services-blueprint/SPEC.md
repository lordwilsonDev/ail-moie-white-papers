# SPEC — AI Business Services Blueprint

## Artifact Contract
This artifact is a business/strategy catalog, not a research or benchmark
artifact. It defines:
1. A categorized service catalog (`BLUEPRINT.md`)
2. A two-track business model: one-time projects + monthly recurring
   revenue (`BLUEPRINT.md`, Business Model section)
3. The rationale for category ordering and growth sequencing
   (`RATIONALE.md`)
4. A phased launch checklist (`CHECKLIST.md`)

## Extending the Catalog
To add a new service:
1. Place it under the most specific existing category in `BLUEPRINT.md`. Add
   a new category only if the service does not fit any of the eleven
   existing ones.
2. If the service can be delivered as a hosted/managed offering, add a
   corresponding line item under **Subscription Services**.
3. If the service is speculative or requires capabilities the business does
   not yet reliably deliver, place it under **Future Expansion** rather than
   a core category.
4. Update `CHECKLIST.md` if the new service changes the recommended launch
   sequence.

## Non-Goals
This artifact does not specify pricing, legal/compliance requirements per
industry vertical, or a technical implementation stack. Those belong in
per-engagement documents that reference this blueprint by category.
